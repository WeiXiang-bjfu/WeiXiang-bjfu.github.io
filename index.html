Biography

I am a Professor and Vice Dean at the School of Forestry, Beijing Forestry University, China. My research focuses on forest growth modelling, natural forest dynamics and sustainable forest management.

I develop statistical and simulation models to understand tree growth, recruitment and mortality, and I study how competition, stand structure, climate and silvicultural treatments shape forest development. My work combines long-term permanent sample plots, tree-ring data and quantitative modelling, with a particular interest in natural and semi-natural mixed forests.

A central goal of my research is to connect quantitative forest science with adaptive and multifunctional management under environmental change.
